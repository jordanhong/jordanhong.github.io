## My Logo
The favicon on this website is a Chinese calligraphy form of my last name (Hong, <span lang="zh-tw">洪</span>). The script used here is called the _small seal script (小篆)_, which dates back to the Eastern Zhou dyasty (approx. 500 BC) and is commonly used in traditional Chinese seals. I think it looks cool. 

I never actually got a chance to specialize in small seal script, despite having practiced Chinese calligraphy since elementary school. Fortunately, my brother Albert Hong (check out his website [!here](https://zzalbert.com/)), specializes in small seal script and offered to designed my favicon for me! Thank you Albert!
